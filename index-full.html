About Streetfoodish

More Than
A Market

Streetfoodish brings together London's most exciting independent street food traders in the heart of the city. From bold international flavours to homegrown talent — every day is different, every bite is real.

We're dedicated to supporting small food businesses, giving talented traders a platform to grow, connect with their community, and do what they love — feed people well.
