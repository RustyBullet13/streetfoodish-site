About Streetfoodish

More Than
A Market

Streetfoodish is London's street food market organiser bringing together the city's most exciting independent traders. From local specialists to international flavours, we curate authentic, energetic markets in the heart of London.

We're dedicated to supporting small food businesses — giving talented traders a platform to grow, connect with customers, and be part of something bigger.
