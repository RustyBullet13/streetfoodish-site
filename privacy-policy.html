01Who We Are

Foodish Limited ("we", "us", "our") is a company registered in England and Wales. We operate street food markets in Central London under the trading name Streetfoodish.

For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, Foodish Limited is the Data Controller responsible for your personal data.

Contact: francesco@streetfoodish.com  |  streetfoodish.com

04Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Where you have given clear consent — for example, permission to use photos and videos of your stall. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us in writing.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): Where processing is necessary for the performance of a contract or to take pre-contractual steps — for example, processing your trader application.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Where we must comply with a legal obligation — for example, retaining records for tax or regulatory purposes.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided these are not overridden by your rights — for example, responding to enquiries and ensuring market safety compliance. We have carried out a balancing test and are satisfied that our interests do not override your fundamental rights.

08Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you (Subject Access Request).
  • Right to rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: Ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restriction: Ask us to restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means.
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests. You have an absolute right to object to direct marketing.
  • Automated decision-making: We do not make automated decisions with significant effects about individuals.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is consent-based. Withdrawal does not affect lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any right, contact us at francesco@streetfoodish.com. We will respond within one calendar month. We may verify your identity before acting on your request. We will not charge a fee for reasonable requests but reserve the right to charge for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

09Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience.

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Required for the website to function. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website. Only set with your consent.
  • Third-party cookies: Embedded content such as Google Maps may set their own cookies. We do not control these — please refer to the relevant third party's privacy policy.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. For more information, visit allaboutcookies.org.

10Marketing Communications

We only send marketing communications with your explicit consent. We do not currently operate a mailing list. Should we introduce marketing communications in future, you will always have the right to opt out by clicking the unsubscribe link or by contacting us directly. Transactional communications (such as application confirmations) are not marketing communications.

11Security

We have implemented appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including HTTPS encryption, secure email via Google Workspace, access controls, and use of reputable third-party processors. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify you and the ICO without undue delay where required by law (within 72 hours).

While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

12Children

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe we hold data from a child under 16, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Our website contains links to third-party websites including social media platforms and Google Maps. These have their own privacy policies for which we take no responsibility. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit. The inclusion of a link does not imply our endorsement of that site.

14Photo & Video Content

Trader applicants are asked to provide explicit, freely given consent for Foodish Limited to use photographs and video of their stall, food, and trading activities for promotional purposes including our website, social media, and press. Refusal to grant consent will not affect your application.

You may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us in writing. Please note that withdrawal does not require removal of already-published content, though we will make reasonable efforts to comply.

Members of the public attending our markets may be photographed or filmed. If you do not wish to appear in such content, please notify a member of our team on the day.
